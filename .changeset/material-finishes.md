Soft, metal and paper finishes are now clearly visible. Buttons show the whole finish instead of a 3px strip (glossy keeps its enamel bands). Soft clay has a pillowy top light and thick inset rim. Metal multiplies a cool steel band into the base colour under an irregular brushed grain and bevel rim. Paper multiplies a warm stock under hairline fibres. Each recipe gains a `blend` value, published as `--dixy-material-<name>-blend`. Metal and paper also have dark-mode variants (gunmetal and kraft) that lighten with `screen` instead of multiplying.
